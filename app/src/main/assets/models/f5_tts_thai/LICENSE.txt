Architecture Specification:
  - Backbone: Diffusion Transformer (DiT) with ConvNeXt V2 conditioning (F5TTS_v1_Base)
  - Hyperparameters: dim=1024, depth=22, heads=16, ff_mult=2, text_dim=512, conv_layers=4
  - Mel-Spectrogram & Vocoder: Vocos 24,000 Hz (n_mel_channels=100, hop_length=256, win_length=1024, n_fft=1024)
  - Tokenizer v1 (F5-TTS-THAI): Character-level UTF-8 vocabulary (vocab_v1.txt, 2,586 tokens)
  - Tokenizer v2 (F5-TTS-TH-V2): Phoneme / IPA vocabulary (vocab_v2.txt, 2,554 tokens)
  - Tokenizer ONNX (F5-TH-ONNX): Thai IPA phoneme vocabulary (f5_onnx_vocab.json, 79 symbols)
  - Reference Conditioning: 24,000 Hz 16-bit Mono PCM reference audio (2-8 seconds) + matching Thai reference transcript
